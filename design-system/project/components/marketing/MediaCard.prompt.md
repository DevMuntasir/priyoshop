Image-led card for press coverage and blog posts. Red uppercase source eyebrow, 3-line clamped title, optional date; lifts on hover.

```jsx
<MediaCard
  image="…"
  source="The Daily Star"
  title="PriyoShop among the top 10 startups in the 'Echelon X' program"
  date="12 Jun 2026"
  href="#"
/>
```

`tone="dark"` for dark sections.
