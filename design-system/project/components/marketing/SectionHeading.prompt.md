Section opener with PriyoShop's signature rhythm: an outline capsule eyebrow above a large Lato title, with optional description and trailing action.

```jsx
<SectionHeading eyebrow="Ecosystem at a Glance" title="Our Business & Ecosystem" />
<SectionHeading
  eyebrow="The Impact"
  title="Bangladesh's Leading Distribution Partner"
  accentWords="Distribution Partner"
  align="left"
  action={<Button>Join Us</Button>}
/>
```

`titleSize` display/h1/h2/h3, `align` center/left, `accentWords` tints a trailing phrase red.
