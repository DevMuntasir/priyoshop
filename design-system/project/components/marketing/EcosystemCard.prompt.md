Two-pane feature card: a pastel copy panel (title + body + ghost CTA) next to a full-bleed brand illustration. The Figma "ecosystem" cards.

```jsx
<EcosystemCard
  title="Retail Business"
  body="B2B platform empowering millions of MSMEs with faster restocking and smart supply chain."
  image="assets/illustrations/shop-storefront.png"
  panelColor="var(--ps-green-tint)"
/>
<EcosystemCard title="Smart Distribution" body="…" panelColor="var(--ps-periwinkle)" reverse />
```

Alternate `panelColor` per card (mint, peach, periwinkle, cyan…) and flip `reverse` for rhythm.
